# Maternity Care Accessibility Atlas

An interactive WebGIS platform for visualizing spatial accessibility to maternity care facilities across the six former Yugoslav republics: Bosnia and Herzegovina, Croatia, Montenegro, North Macedonia, Serbia, and Slovenia.

The platform provides interactive maps of driving-time accessibility to maternity care facilities, enabling users to explore spatial differences in healthcare accessibility across the study region.

## Associated Publication

This WebGIS platform was developed as a supplementary resource for the following scientific paper, which is currently being prepared for publication:

Arnaut, F., Rauch, S., Pantović, L., & Jevremović, S. (in preparation). Maternity care within reach: Mapping spatial accessibility and regional inequalities across the former Yugoslav republics.

The complete citation and DOI will be provided upon publication.

## Features

- **Interactive WebGIS map** displaying spatial accessibility to maternity care facilities.
- **Two accessibility scenarios:**
  - **30-minute scenario:** 25-, 30-, and 35-minute driving-time accessibility, including areas beyond 35 minutes.
  - **65-minute scenario:** 55-, 60-, and 65-minute driving-time accessibility, including areas beyond 65 minutes.
- **Country- specific layer controls** for enabling or disabling individual countries and datasets.
- **Maternity facility locations** displayed in both accessibility scenarios.
- **Administrative boundaries** at national and regional levels.
- **Interactive statistical results table** containing population accessibility estimates.
- **Downloadable spatial datasets** for further research and analysis.
- **Study information panel** containing publication details.

## Study Area

The analysis covers six countries:

1. Bosnia and Herzegovina (Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina, Republika Srpska and the Brčko district)
2. Croatia
3. Montenegro
4. North Macedonia
5. Serbia
6. Slovenia

## Usage

The interactive atlas can be accessed through the GitHub Pages website associated with this repository.

Users can select between the two accessibility scenarios, enable or disable geographical groups and individual layers, inspect maternity facility locations, and explore statistical results.

The underlying spatial datasets can be downloaded for independent analysis.
